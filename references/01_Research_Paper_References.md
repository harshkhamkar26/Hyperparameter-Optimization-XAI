## Research-Gap Conclusion

The literature already contains Bank Marketing + class balancing, Bank Marketing + HPO, and Bank Marketing + SHAP, including recent studies combining several of these components. Therefore, the project's contribution should be positioned as a **controlled and reproducible comparison of HPO strategy × imbalance treatment × predictive performance × computational efficiency × explanation stability**, rather than as a claim that HPO or SHAP has never been applied to Bank Marketing.

## Paper-Study Template

For each paper, record: problem, gap, dataset, preprocessing, models, HPO strategy, objective, imbalance treatment, metrics, XAI method, stability/robustness method, results, limitations, and claimed contribution.
