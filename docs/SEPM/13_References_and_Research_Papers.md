## 7. Research Positioning

Recent IDS literature already demonstrates explainable IDS, SHAP-based explanations and even combinations of Optuna, LightGBM and SHAP. Therefore, the proposed project must not claim that HPO + SHAP is itself novel.

The proposed research contribution is the controlled empirical study of:

**HPO strategy × imbalance treatment × IDS performance × computational efficiency × explanation stability**

The final paper should explicitly distinguish the contribution from existing explainable IDS and HPO-based IDS studies.

## 8. Verification Rule
Before final paper submission, verify all bibliographic metadata, DOI, venue, publication year and dataset citations against the publisher or official dataset source.
